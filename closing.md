After careful consideration, we have made the difficult decision to close
Badger. **The service will end operations on October 31, 2026.**

We want to thank every agency, freelancer and marketer who trusted Badger to
build and share their reports. It has been a privilege to be part of your
workflow.

### What happens now

Badger will keep working as usual until the closing date. You can continue to
log in, connect your sources and generate reports until then.

New signups are already closed, and no new subscriptions or renewals will be
charged.

### Key dates

- **Today:** New signups are closed. Existing accounts keep working normally.
- **October 31, 2026:** Badger stops operating. Access to the application and
  all reports ends on this date.
- **November 30, 2026:** All customer data is permanently deleted from our
  systems.

### Your data

Your data belongs to you. Before the closing date, we recommend that you:

- Download or print the reports you want to keep.
- Export any charts, annotations or comments you still need.
- Disconnect any data sources you no longer want linked to Badger.

After the closing date, all remaining data will be permanently deleted in
accordance with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/). We will not keep copies.

### Your subscription

If you have an active paid plan, you will not be charged again. Any prepaid
period that extends beyond the closing date will be refunded on a pro rata
basis. You do not need to do anything; refunds will be issued automatically to
your original payment method.

### Need help?

If you have questions about your account, your data or your refund, please
write to us at [badger@marketgoo.com](mailto:badger@marketgoo.com). We will
keep answering emails until the closing date and for a short period after.

Thank you again for being part of Badger.

*The Badger team*
